## TL;DR — the five things that decide the build

1. **Sell to the local section, not to ESN International.** ESN is a three-tier federation
   (International AISBL in Brussels → National orgs → ~500–600 local sections in ~45 countries).
   Governance devolved to national orgs in 2019. A **local section president/board can say yes
   on their own** to using a free tool. An "official ESN partnership" is a slow, Brussels-gated,
   revenue-sharing corporate process (Ryanair, FlixBus) — **not** our path.
2. **The unmet job is pre-arrival _community/connection_ — nobody owns it.** BuddySystem /
   Papaya / Broaddy do buddy _matching_. The official Erasmus+ App does _admin/checklists_.
   Facebook/WhatsApp groups do _chat_ but are unstructured, often non-ESN, and gated. There is
   **no structured, section-branded "your people are already here" layer before arrival.**
3. **The universal funnel is `Instagram → link-in-bio → scattered destinations`.** Every section
   runs discovery through Instagram and a Linktree/Satellite bio link that fans out to a buddy
   form, a WhatsApp group, the ESNcard, and a survival guide. **The install path is to become
   the single smart link in that bio.**
4. **Design for annual volunteer turnover and zero budget.** One-year board terms → the tool must
   be zero-training, self-explanatory, effortless to hand over, and free. If it needs a power-user
   to run it, it dies when that student graduates.
5. **Data privacy is the #1 objection.** A volunteer handing incoming-student contact data to an
   outside startup is the fear. Every big ESN partnership reached students **without** handing over
   raw data (value flowed through the ESNcard as a verified gate, or opt-in platforms).
   **Students must opt in themselves; the section never exports a list.** This single design choice
   neutralizes the central "no."

## 1. How ESN is structured

| Tier | Who | What they control | Relevance to us |
|---|---|---|---|
| **International** (AISBL, Brussels) | 5-person International Board (1-yr terms) + ~10 paid secretariat incl. a Partnership Manager | EU advocacy, brand, corporate partnerships, shared IT (Galaxy, ESNcard) | Too slow/gated for a free tool. Avoid at start. |
| **National** (ESN Spain, ESN Italia, ESN France, …) | National Board + Fundraising/Partnerships committee | National partners applying to all sections in-country; ratifies new sections | The scale path _after_ we've landed sections. |
| **Local sections** (~500–600, ~45 countries) | Local Board: **President, VP, Treasurer, Secretary**, + Comms/Webmaster, + Buddy & Partnerships roles | Buddy programme, welcome week, local partners, **the tools they use for incoming students** | **This is the customer.** A president can adopt us unilaterally. |

- Scale (figures conflict across ESN's own pages — verify at esn.org/sections): **~500–600 sections
  in ~45 countries**, **~15,000 active volunteers**, serving **~220,000–350,000 international
  students/year**, present in **900–1,000+ institutions**.
- Multi-section-per-city is real: **Barcelona has four sections** under one umbrella (`@esn_bcn` →
  ESN UAB, UB, UPC, UPF). The data model must support **one city → several university sections**.
- Operating cadence is **semester-driven**: every section runs a **Welcome Week** at each semester
  start — the twice-a-year peak-load moment. Identity frame: the **"Erasmus Generation,"** operating
  principle **"Students Helping Students."**
- **Annual turnover** of one-year boards means institutional knowledge is rebuilt yearly.

## 2. What sections use for pre-arrival today (fragmented)

- **Buddy matching is split across ≥3 competing back-ends** — no ESN-wide standard:
  - **BuddySystem** (buddysystem.eu) — built 2013 by four students in Lille, spread through **ESN
    France**; 100k+ users. Coordinator **manually** ranks & pairs. Strongest in France.
  - **Papaya** (iter-idea, from ESN Modena) — auto-matches by interests; common in **Italy**.
  - **Broaddy** — locals pick buddies from a list; common in **Czechia/Croatia**.
  - **DIY Google Forms** everywhere else.
  - _All are matching engines, not pre-arrival community. Interoperate — don't rebuild._
- **ESN's own platforms:** **ESN Galaxy** (volunteer intranet + "Satellite" website template),
  **ESNcard** (membership + 890+ discounts incl. Ryanair/FlixBus — the commercial backbone),
  the **official Erasmus+ App** (pre-departure checklists, OLA status — admin, not community),
  **SocialErasmus** & **Mov'in Europe** (campaigns, not logistics tools).
- **How incoming students find each other today:** unstructured & student-initiated — city Facebook
  groups ("Erasmus Barcelona 2025/26", often non-ESN/commercial), per-city WhatsApp/Telegram groups
  linked from those, Instagram link-in-bio, and long survival-guide pages / Welcome Books
  (ESN Lisboa's is ~100 pages). **No canonical entry point.**

## 3. Section-volunteer pain points (our ROI story)

- **Repeated high-volume questions every semester:** housing, local SIM, public transport,
  airport→city, university registration/bureaucracy, health insurance, bank accounts. The info exists
  but is restated endlessly across channels.
- **Twice-a-year peak load** compresses hundreds of arrivals into Welcome Week; "volunteers with
  limited time and resources."
- **Manual buddy-matching** admin at the busiest moment; tools are incompatible across countries.
- **Burnout + annual turnover** (first-year volunteer retention ~55%); onboarding knowledge rebuilt
  yearly. _(Burnout specifics lean on general volunteer-management research applied to ESN's
  confirmed structure.)_
- **Fragmented tools / no single source of truth**; student contact data scattered.
- **Students land unconnected** — discovery is unstructured and matching happens late.

## 4. How ESN partnerships work (and why ours is grassroots)

- **ESNcard = the commercial backbone.** ~200k cards/year; partners plug in at **local, national,
  or international** tier. Value flows through the card as a **verified-eligibility gate** — partners
  get routing, **not a raw student list**.
- **Ryanair (the flagship template):** 9-year annual renewal; student gets 10% off 4 flights + free
  20kg bag, **but only through a gated "Erasmus" widget after validating ESN membership on
  ESNcard.org**. ESN gets a headline benefit + fundraising revenue; Ryanair gets verified access to a
  young high-frequency-travel segment. **Mechanic to steal: value through a verified gate, never a
  data dump.** Others: FlixBus, Interrail/Eurail, Eurosender, Uniplaces, HousingAnywhere (via the
  EU-funded Project HOME consortium). _(DFDS/Booking.com unconfirmed.)_
- **Approval by tier:** international = Board + AGM + secretariat, multi-month, for paying corporates.
  National = National Board + partnerships committee. **Local = a president/board vote — a
  15-minute agenda item.**
- **What a section can do alone:** run its buddy programme, sign local partners, **choose the tools it
  uses for welcome week / incoming students, and promote them.** **What it cannot do:** bind ESN
  nationally, call a third party an "official ESN partner," or wrap it in the **EU-registered ESN logo**
  (governed by the Visual Identity Manual). → **We may be a section's chosen tool and co-brand with the
  _section's own_ identity; we may NOT claim to be an "official ESN partner" or use the ESN logo.**

### Precedents

- **BuddySystem** — our closest analog _and_ rival. A free, student-built pre-arrival tool that spread
  **grassroots, university-by-university through ESN France**, not via a Brussels contract. Proof the
  path works; position **complementary**, not a replacement.
- **Uniplaces** — corporate BD with Brussels, then network rollout (has revenue to share; not us).
- **HousingAnywhere** — via an EU grant consortium (slow, deep; not us).
- **Erasmusu** — grew a _parallel_ Erasmus community outside ESN, acquired by Spotahome (2018). Shows
  the audience is reachable without ESN — but ESN's trusted on-campus distribution is what these
  never replicated.
- **Pattern:** free tool solving section pain → **grassroots, section-by-section** (our bucket).

## 5. Go-to-market for 3–5 sections this semester

- **Grassroots, bottom-up.** Approach individual section presidents/boards who can approve now.
- **Target profile:** big-inbound universities in high-Erasmus cities (Spain, Italy, Portugal,
  Netherlands, Germany). Prefer sections **not** locked into BuddySystem (or position as complementary
  in France). Warm-start with a board member who did an exchange and remembers pre-arrival anxiety —
  your natural champion. Land one, get a testimonial + usage numbers, use it to reach neighboring
  sections in the same national org (the BuddySystem playbook).
- **Timing:** 8–10 weeks before Welcome Week. **It is 2026-08-21 — fall Welcome Weeks are ~2–6 weeks
  out.** The MVP must ship in **days**, and we must do 100% of setup for the section.
- **What to offer:** free forever, **zero setup burden** (we configure everything), **opt-in by
  students — section never exports data**, a **section-branded page/link that feels like theirs**, and a
  simple usage view they can show their university International Office.
- **What to avoid:** don't claim "official ESN partner"/use the ESN logo; don't ask for a student list
  or be vague about the business model; don't position as a BuddySystem replacement where it's used.
- **The pitch:** _"A free tool that lets your incoming students connect with each other and your section
  **before they arrive** — so your volunteers spend Welcome Week welcoming people instead of answering
  the same 200 WhatsApp questions. Students opt in themselves; you never touch their data or spend a
  euro. Set up in 10 minutes, live before your next intake."_
